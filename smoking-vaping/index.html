How the estimate works

Daily cost = cost per unit × units used ÷ days in the usage period. Weekly cost uses 7 days; annual cost uses 365 days. Longer totals assume the same price and use each year. Savings are avoided product spending, without inflation, interest, replacement purchases, or healthcare costs.
